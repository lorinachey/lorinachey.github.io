On April 2nd, I received notification that I had been selected as a 2025 NDSEG Fellowship recipient! I'm thrilled to
have the support of this fellowship during the remainder of my PhD program. Having this fellowship will allow
me to focus on my research without having to worry about my PhD funding and/or having to jump
from project to project when I need additional funding. This is such an incredible achievement. I'm so thankful
for the professors and my lab colleagues who helped me draft the research proposal and spent countless hours helping me
revise and edit my application materials before submission. Without them, this would not have been possible!
