### Thesis research
I want autonomous agents to know their own competence and to take risk tolerance as an explicit input, and I want both to still hold once the system leaves the conditions it was calibrated in: when it is compressed, physically embodied, degraded, or handed to a new user. My work characterizes where competence and risk guarantees break between calibration and deployment, and develops agents whose risk behaviour stays calibrated under those conditions. One part of this uses distributional reinforcement learning to learn locomotion and navigation policies for quadrupeds that account for risk directly.

### Secondary research interests
Alongside the thesis I work on diffusion-based occupancy prediction for exploration and mapping, predicting where objects belong in a home from a person's routine, and communication-aware multi-agent exploration. The [research page]({% link pages/research.md %}) has the papers.
