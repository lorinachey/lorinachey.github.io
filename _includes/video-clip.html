{%- comment -%} A short self-hosted clip that plays silently in a loop, where a GIF would once have gone. Progressive enhancement: without JavaScript it is a poster with native controls; with video-clip.js it starts when scrolled into view, unless the visitor prefers reduced motion, in which case it stays a still. The clip is sized by its own width/height so a wide side-by-side recording is not forced into the 16:9 poster slot the YouTube cards use. Usage: {% include video-clip.html clip=proj.clip %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign c = include.clip -%}
